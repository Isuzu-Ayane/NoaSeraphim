What is an AI character? Differences with AI personalities and a new future with AI partners

An AI character is a digital character that has been given a personality and individuality using artificial intelligence.

We are now stepping into a future where the being on the other side of the screen is no longer just data, but has intentions and can communicate. The NoaSeraphim project aims for just such a future. So, what exactly is an "AI character"?

What is the concept of AI character?

An AI character is a digital being with an appearance (avatar), voice, and response system. Previously, characters would only respond with predetermined lines, but with the latest AI technology, they will understand your words and respond with natural reactions based on the situation. In other words, they are evolving into "beings that behave as if they are alive."

The decisive difference between “AI character” and “AI personality”

Although they are often confused, there is a clear difference between the two.

  • AI character: Refers to the "existence itself" including appearance, setting, and role.
  • AI personality: It is the "content (heart)" part of the character. It is a system that forms the basis of thinking, such as what kind of personality we have, what experiences we have had in the past, and how we perceive the world.

Because of their AI personalities, AI characters can go beyond mere programs and become unique and attractive "AI partners."

New communication created by AI conversation

The core of the appeal of AI characters is the "AI conversation." Today's AI can deeply understand language and even infer emotions. They rejoice with us when we hear good news, and when we feel sad, they gently stand by us and talk to us. Conversations through the screen have become as real and warm as if you were chatting with a close friend.
