    Useful Quality Embeddings
    aid210.pt     		Other
    A negative embedding designed for Anime Illust Diffusion v2.10, but effective with other models as well.
    It improves image quality without excessively compromising the base model's original style. Key effects include adding soft light and shadow, emphasizing contour lines (brush strokes), improving sharpness, removing artifacts and noise, correcting line distortion, and slightly beautifying faces.
    Compared to the similar embedding 'aid291', it has a gentler correction effect, resulting in a more natural finish.

    araizumi rui.pt     		Other

    Bad-Hands-5
    bad-hands-5.pt     		Other
    A negative embedding specifically designed to fix and reduce common "hand deformations" in AI image generation (e.g., incorrect number of fingers, unnatural joints). By specifying this in the negative prompt, it becomes easier to render more natural and anatomically correct hands. Highly valued for character illustrations and portrait generation.
    badhandv4
    badhandv4.pt     		Other
    A negative text embedding developed to fix common issues with hand rendering in AI image generation. It is designed to improve hand quality while minimizing the impact on the overall artistic style and background of the image. It is more effective when used with a high CFG scale (e.g., 11 or higher) and can be used in combination with other negative embeddings.
