What Specs Do You Need for AI?

← Back to Guides
You don't necessarily need a high-end PC to run Stable Diffusion, LLMs, and AI image generation locally.

Which parts should you invest in? What are the minimum requirements?

We've summarized the key points in order of importance.

1Memory (RAM)

AI processing loads massive amount of model data into memory. Insufficient capacity leads to sluggishness or crashes.

Recommended Specs:
Minimum 32GB. For professional use, 64GB or more is highly recommended.

2GPU (Graphics Card)

The heart of AI computation. NVIDIA (GeForce) is the industry standard.

VRAM (Video Memory) What you can do
8GB Basic Image Gen (SD 1.5)
12GB High-quality Gen (SDXL) / LoRA Training
16GB or more Faster LLM (Chat AI) / Large-scale training

*Priority rule: VRAM capacity is more important than GPU generation (30XX vs 40XX) for AI tasks.

3CPU

GPU does the heavy lifting, so CPU just needs to be fast enough not to be a bottleneck.

Recommended Class:
Intel Core i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 or higher from recent generations.

4Storage

AI models are huge (GBs each). Read speed directly affects your experience.

Recommended: NVMe SSD (1TB+ recommended)
