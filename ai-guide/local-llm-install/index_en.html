Easiest Local LLM
Setup Guide

A safe, private AI chat environment running entirely on your own PC. Start right now with LM Studio.

STEP 1

Download App

Get "LM Studio," widely regarded as the most user-friendly local LLM tool in the world.

Official LM Studio Site

Available with a single click.

STEP 2

Install and First Launch

Simply run the downloaded file; setup completes in seconds. No complex environment configuration required.

STEP 3

Search and Get AI Models

Search for AI models (Llama, Mistral, etc.) directly within the app.

Quick Tip Models labeled with Q4_K_M in their names are highly recommended for the best balance of speed and intelligence.
STEP 4

Start Chatting

Select the "Chat" (speech bubble) icon from the left menu, choose your downloaded model from the top, and you're set! Ask anything you like.

🤖 Setup Complete!

You can now chat with AI anytime while keeping your privacy intact.

Try introducing your favorite AI characters for more advanced interactions.
