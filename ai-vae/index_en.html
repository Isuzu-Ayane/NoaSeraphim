[Magician of Color]
Use VAE to dramatically change the "transparency" and "density" of your illustrations!

When generating AI images, have you ever felt that the colors are too pale? or that the whole image is whitish and blurry?

The problem is actually not the fault of the model (Checkpoint).
One setting of ``VAE'' may solve the problem!

By using the references on this site, you will no longer have to worry about choosing a VAE.

🌟 Why do I need to switch VAE?

1. Eliminate "color cast" and "dullness"!

SD 1.5系などは、VAEを入れないと色が全体的にグレーがかってしまいます。適切なVAE（Anime2 や mse840k など）をセットするだけで、パッと目の前が開けるような鮮やかな発色に変わります。

2. The sharpness of the lines changes!

VAE is the final process of "decoding (assembling images)". Choosing a high-quality VAE will dramatically improve the sharpness of fine details and contour lines.

3. Prevent “black images (NaN errors)”!

This is a generation error that is particularly likely to occur in SDXL and Pony environments. By using a dedicated VAE (such as fixFP16Errors), stable generation is possible while avoiding errors.
